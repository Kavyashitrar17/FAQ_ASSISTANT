Airtel Customer Support FAQ Knowledge Base

1. What is Airtel?
Airtel is one of India’s leading telecommunications companies providing mobile network services, broadband internet, DTH television, and digital payment solutions. Airtel offers prepaid and postpaid mobile plans along with 4G and 5G connectivity services across India.

2. How can I recharge my Airtel prepaid number?
You can recharge your Airtel prepaid number using multiple methods:
- Airtel Thanks App
- Google Pay
- PhonePe
- Paytm
- Net Banking
- Debit/Credit Card
- Nearby recharge shops

To recharge using the Airtel Thanks App:
1. Open the app
2. Select Recharge
3. Enter your mobile number
4. Choose a recharge plan
5. Complete payment

3. How do I check my Airtel balance?
You can check your Airtel balance by:
- Dialing *123#
- Using the Airtel Thanks App
- Calling customer care

The balance details include:
- Main balance
- Data balance
- SMS balance
- Plan validity

4. How can I activate Airtel 5G?
Airtel 5G activates automatically if:
- Your smartphone supports 5G
- You are in a 5G coverage area
- Your SIM is 5G enabled

Steps to verify:
1. Enable 5G in mobile settings
2. Restart your phone
3. Ensure mobile data is ON

5. What should I do if mobile internet is slow?
If your Airtel internet is slow:
- Restart your phone
- Turn airplane mode ON/OFF
- Check network signal strength
- Reset network settings
- Ensure data limit is not exhausted

If the problem continues:
- Contact Airtel support at 121
- Visit the nearest Airtel store

6. How can I contact Airtel customer care?
Customer care options:
- Dial 121 for general support
- Dial 198 for complaints
- Use Airtel Thanks App live chat
- Visit official Airtel website
- Visit nearby Airtel store

7. How can I change my Airtel SIM card?
To replace your SIM card:
1. Visit nearest Airtel store
2. Carry valid ID proof
3. Request SIM replacement
4. Complete verification process

The new SIM usually activates within a few hours.

8. What should I do if my SIM is lost?
If your SIM is lost:
1. Call Airtel customer care immediately
2. Block the SIM card
3. Visit Airtel store for replacement
4. Carry identity proof

This prevents unauthorized use of your number.

9. How can I port my number to Airtel?
To switch to Airtel:
1. Send SMS:
PORT <mobile number>
to 1900

2. Receive UPC code
3. Visit Airtel store
4. Submit documents
5. Choose a plan

Porting usually completes within a few days.
